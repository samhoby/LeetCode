# 175. Combine Two Tables

- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Topics:** [Database](../README.md#database)
- **Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/combine-two-tables/

## Key idea
Every person has to be in the result, even the ones with no address. A left join keeps every row of `Person` and fills `city` and `state` with null when there's no matching row in `Address`. An inner join would drop the people without an address.

Example: if Allen Wang has no address and Bob Alice lives in New York City, New York, the result is Allen Wang with null and null, and Bob Alice with New York City and New York.

## Pattern
Left join to keep every row of the left table, matched on the shared key (`personId`).

## Complexity
O(n + m) time, O(m) space with a hash join, where n is the number of rows in `Person` and m in `Address`.
