# 21. Merge Two Sorted Lists

- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Topics:** [Linked List](../README.md#linked-list), [Recursion](../README.md#recursion)
- **Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/merge-two-sorted-lists/

## Key idea
Both lists are sorted, so the smallest node left is always at the front of one of them. Keep taking the smaller of the two fronts and linking it after the last node of the result. When one list runs out, the rest of the other one is already sorted and not smaller than anything taken, so it's linked as a whole. No new nodes are made, only the links between the existing ones change.

Example with `list1 = [1,2,4]` and `list2 = [1,3,4]`: the fronts are compared and the smaller one is taken each time, giving `[1,1,2,3,4,4]`.

This solution is iterative. The Recursion topic is for another approach: the smaller front node is followed by the merge of everything after it with the other list.

## Pattern
Two pointers over two sorted lists, with a dummy node before the result so the first node doesn't need a special case. The answer is the node after the dummy.

## Complexity
O(n + m) time, O(1) space, where n and m are the lengths of `list1` and `list2`.
