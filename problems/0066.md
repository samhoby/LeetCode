# 66. Plus One

- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Topics:** [Array](../README.md#array), [Math](../README.md#math)
- **Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/plus-one/

## Key idea
Add 1 the way it's done by hand, starting from the last digit. A digit below 9 just goes up by 1, and the addition is done. A 9 becomes 0 and carries the 1 to the digit on its left. If every digit was 9, they all become 0 and the carry needs a new digit in front, so the answer is a 1 followed by zeros, one digit longer.

Example with `digits = [1,2,9]`: the 9 becomes 0 and carries 1, so the 2 becomes 3, giving `[1,3,0]`. With `[9,9]`, both become 0 and a 1 goes in front, giving `[1,0,0]`.

## Pattern
Simulate the addition from right to left with a carry, and stop as soon as there's nothing left to carry.

## Complexity
O(n) time, O(1) space.
