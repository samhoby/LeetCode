# 135. Candy

- **Difficulty:** Hard
- **Topics:** [Array](../README.md#array), [Greedy](../README.md#greedy)
- **Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/candy/

## Key idea
Every child gets at least 1 candy, and a child with a higher rating than a neighbour must get more than that neighbour. The two neighbours can be handled separately. Going left to right, a child rated higher than the one on the left gets one more candy than that one. Then going right to left, a child rated higher than the one on the right needs one more than that one, but keeps the bigger of the two amounts, so the left rule still holds. Each child ends with the smallest amount that works for both sides, so the total is the minimum.

Example with `ratings = [1,0,2]`: left to right gives `[1,1,2]`. Right to left, the first child is rated higher than the second, so it needs 2, giving `[2,1,2]`. The answer is 5.

## Pattern
Greedy in two passes, one for each direction, keeping the bigger value where they meet.

## Complexity
O(n) time, O(n) space.
